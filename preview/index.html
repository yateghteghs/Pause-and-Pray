Browser preview

Pause & Pray

Pause and pray before you scroll.

This is the real app running in your browser. A browser cannot pause other apps, so the preview uses sample apps instead.

Try this

  1. Create an account (the preview keeps it in your browser), choose three intentions, set a prayer time and choose Instagram.
  2. On Today, tap Make it prayer time now. Your apps are now paused until you pray.
  3. Tap Open Instagram: there is no skip, only I've prayed, which turns on after 5 seconds.
  4. Open Groups and join with the code MONICA to see a sample group praying together.

Only on a phone

  • Pausing your real apps (Android Accessibility Service, iPhone Screen Time)
  • Reminders at the Angelus and the 3pm Hour of Mercy

To start over: Me, then Privacy and your data, then Delete all my data.
